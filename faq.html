Frequently Asked Questions

What are your guitar bodies made from?

JGV3D bodies are 3D-printed using durable, performance-oriented materials designed for guitars and basses. Most builds are produced in PETG or PLA+ depending on the model and application, and the materials are selected for strength, stability, and everyday playability.

Are these bodies custom-made?

Many of our bodies are made to order, and some models are kept in stock when available. Custom finishes, paint colors, routed layouts, and unique configurations are available depending on the build and the product.

Do you offer custom paint or color requests?

Yes. Custom color requests and finish notes can be included during the ordering process. If you have a specific look in mind, we recommend sharing the details in the custom notes section so your order can be reviewed before checkout.

How long does production usually take?

Production times vary by model and order volume. In general, made-to-order builds are completed as quickly as possible, and we do our best to keep lead times transparent and realistic for each product.

Do these bodies work with standard hardware?

Most JGV3D bodies are designed to be compatible with standard guitar hardware and common pickup configurations. However, some custom builds may require specific neck, bridge, or routing details, so it is best to review the product notes and customization options before ordering.

What if I want a different body shape, routing, or finish?

That is exactly the kind of request we are built for. If you are looking for a non-standard layout, finish, or a unique body concept, contact us through the site or email and we can talk through the possibilities before you place an order.

How are orders handled and tracked?

Orders are tracked locally in the site during checkout and can be viewed from the Orders page. The current status system is designed to show the order lifecycle from queue to completion while the shop continues to grow and add more automated backend support.

Do you ship worldwide?

Shipping availability depends on the destination and current order volume. If you have a question about shipping to your area, reach out directly and we can help confirm the best option for your order.
