User activity

Each signed-in visitor records their own email, last sign-in time and last activity time (at most every 15 minutes) in Firestore userActivity/{uid}. Only admins can read it. Users who haven't signed in since this was deployed won't appear.

Account orders (Firestore)

Default scope: your signed-in admin account's own orders. Select ALL accounts explicitly to manage other customers' orders. Orders placed by signed-in buyers are saved at users/{uid}/orders/{orderId}. Guests can't place orders. Item prices and totals come from the buyer's browser; verify them before taking payment. Refresh loads a current snapshot, not a live subscription.

Status changes and deletion affect order records only: no refunds, payments, shipping notifications or stock adjustments. Private JSON exports contain customer data: download only when needed, keep them secure, and never share them publicly.
